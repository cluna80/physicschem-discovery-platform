🔬 System Overview

The Quantum Drug Discovery Lab is an AI-powered quantum computing platform that discovers novel drug candidates using Grover's algorithm. The system achieves 125x theoretical speedup over classical search.

⚛️ Quantum Simulator

The simulator runs Grover's algorithm on 4-28 qubits, demonstrating the exponential sensitivity to noise:

  • 4 qubits: 94.5% success
  • 10 qubits: 81.3% success
  • 15 qubits: 64.6% success
  • 20 qubits: 3.7% success (catastrophic collapse)
  • 28 qubits: 0.0038% success

Conclusion: 5% noise kills quantum algorithms by 20 qubits.

🧬 Drug Discovery Pipeline

  1. Generate Novel Molecules: Using chemical fragments and functional groups
  2. RDKit Validation: Calculate real properties (MW, LogP, QED, Lipinski)
  3. Binding Prediction: QSAR-based prediction for 7 disease targets
  4. Quantum Search: 125x speedup using Grover's algorithm
  5. Library Storage: All discoveries saved permanently
