What is Grover's Algorithm?
Grover's algorithm searches an unsorted database of N items in O(√N) steps vs O(N) classically. For your drug library of 128 candidates: classical = 128 queries, quantum = ~11 queries. Quadratic speedup.

In this lab it ranks drug candidates by binding affinity using quantum amplitude amplification — the best binders are amplified and measured with high probability.
